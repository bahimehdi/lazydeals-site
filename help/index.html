Get started

  1. Install LazyDeals from a supported browser store and open the full-page app from the popup.
  2. Choose All Stores or Regional pricing. Only your active mode runs background checks.
  3. Search for a game, add it to the shared watchlist and set a target for that mode.

LazyDeals sends an alert when an eligible game price first falls below its target or reaches a new lower qualifying price. Keep the browser’s extension notifications enabled.

Choose a pricing mode

All Stores checks your preferred stores against targets in USD. Regional uses Steam-returned prices and currencies for Steam-linked games. Regional targets are compared only with the same returned currency; there is no conversion or country selector.

Switching modes does not delete your targets, but a game needs its own target in the active mode to be monitored there.

Import a shared Steam wishlist

  1. Open your wishlist in Steam and choose Share.
  2. Copy the Share link and paste it into LazyDeals’ wishlist import field.
  3. Preview the matches, review each target and save the games you want to monitor.

The import is manual. Use Update when you want to review a newer shared wishlist view; LazyDeals does not stay connected to your Steam account or automatically remove watched games you later remove in Steam.

Some wishlist games cannot be matched exactly. An adult-rated game can appear among unmatched entries, but its rating alone does not establish why no match exists. Unmatched entries cannot be monitored yet.

Set a budget bundle

Select at least two watched games with targets in your current mode, then set one total budget for the group. All Stores uses the cheapest eligible offer per game from your preferred stores; those offers may be from different stores. Regional uses comparable Steam prices in one currency. Bundles are shopping plans, not one checkout.

Each bundle can keep a separate budget in each pricing mode. If you remove a watched game, LazyDeals removes it from the bundle and pauses that bundle’s alerts until you review it.

Reported 100%-off offers

The full-page app shows normally paid PC games reported at 100% off. The rail remains visible even if you turn its separate notifications off. Reports are not guaranteed: an offer may have ended or may not apply in your region or account. Confirm it on the store page before claiming.

If an alert or price looks wrong

Check that the correct pricing mode is active, the game has a target in that mode and browser notifications are allowed. Regional checks need a comparable Steam price in the same currency. Store prices and reported offers can change between checks.
